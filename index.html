Majlis Clips

The publishing tool of Bazm e Rahat e Sukhan · بزم راحت سخن

Majlis Clips turns the Bazm's own recordings of its majalis — Urdu salam, soz and marsiya — into short reels and full-length videos, with the verses as captions, and posts them to the Bazm's own pages after a person has approved each one.

Find the Bazm on Facebook as Bazm E Rahat E Sukhan / بزم راحت سخن, on Instagram as @rahat_e_sukhan, and on YouTube.
