Majlis Clips · effective 18 October 2026

What Majlis Clips is

Majlis Clips is a private tool of Bazm e Rahat e Sukhan (بزم راحت سخن). It turns the Bazm's own recordings of its majalis into reels and videos and, when one of the Bazm's administrators clicks a button for a specific video, uploads that video to the Bazm's own Facebook Page, Instagram account or YouTube channel.

Who may use it

Only the people who run the Bazm's pages. There are no public accounts, and access to the tool is given by the Bazm.

Your accounts on other platforms

By granting the tool access to a Facebook Page, an Instagram account or a YouTube channel, you confirm you are allowed to manage it. The tool uploads only what you choose and only to that account. You can remove its access at any time in the platform's settings; see the data deletion instructions. Your use of those platforms remains under their own terms (Meta's Terms, YouTube's Terms of Service).

Content

The recordings, verses and videos belong to the Bazm and to the poets and reciters who recited them. The tool writes captions exactly as recited and never changes the poetry. You are responsible for what you choose to publish.
