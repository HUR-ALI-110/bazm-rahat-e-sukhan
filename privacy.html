Privacy policy

Majlis Clips · effective 18 October 2026

Who we are

Majlis Clips is an internal tool run by Bazm e Rahat e Sukhan (بزم راحت سخن), a community that organises majalis of Urdu religious poetry. The tool is used only by the people who run the Bazm's pages. It is not offered to the public and has no accounts for the public.

What the tool does

It takes the Bazm's own recordings of its gatherings, finds the verses in them, adds the verses as captions, and — only when one of us clicks a button for a specific video — uploads that video with its caption to the Bazm's own Facebook Page, Instagram account or YouTube channel. Nothing is posted automatically.

Data the tool accesses on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube

  • Access tokens that a Page administrator grants to it, so it can upload to the Bazm's own Page, Instagram professional account and YouTube channel.
  • For each video it uploads: the post's ID and link, and the platform's processing and copyright-check status for that post.

It does not read anyone's profile, friends, followers, messages, comments or feed. It does not collect data about the people who watch the videos. Viewer statistics are seen only in the platforms' own tools, not in Majlis Clips.

Other data the tool holds

  • The Bazm's own recordings and the videos made from them.
  • Transcripts of the poetry recited in those recordings. Short audio pieces of the Bazm's own public recordings are sent to an AI transcription service (Google Gemini, through OpenRouter) to write down the verses exactly as recited. These recordings are of public gatherings; no data about audience members is sent.
  • The names of the reciters as they appear in the Bazm's own programmes, to credit them in the posts.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Everything is stored on the Bazm's own computer and its own database; access tokens are kept in a protected folder on that computer and are never shown or sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them. Records of posts are kept for as long as the Bazm keeps its archive. Tokens are deleted when the Bazm stops using the tool or revokes its access (see below).

Sharing

Data is shared only with the platforms the Bazm posts to (Meta's Facebook and Instagram, Google's YouTube), with the transcription service named above, and with Google Drive for the Bazm's own backups. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Cookies and tracking

These pages set no cookies, run no analytics and load nothing from other sites. The tool itself is a private application used only inside the Bazm.

Your choices

  • A Page administrator can revoke the tool's access at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Majlis Clips → Remove. For YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services.
  • Anyone can ask us to delete data that concerns them: see the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Message the Bazm's Facebook Page, Bazm E Rahat E Sukhan / بزم راحت سخن, or Instagram @rahat_e_sukhan. We answer within seven days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
