Data deletion instructions

Majlis Clips · Bazm e Rahat e Sukhan

Majlis Clips holds no data about people who watch the Bazm's videos. The only personal data it can hold is the access that a Page administrator grants it, and the names of reciters credited in the Bazm's posts. Here is how each is removed.

1. Remove the tool's access to your Facebook or Instagram account

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Choose Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Majlis Clips and press Remove. Meta then deletes the access token on its side and tells us; we delete our copy within seven days.

For YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services → Majlis Clips → Remove access.

2. Ask us to delete data that concerns you

Send a message to the Bazm's Facebook Page, Bazm E Rahat E Sukhan / بزم راحت سخن, or to Instagram @rahat_e_sukhan, saying “delete my data” and what it concerns — for example your name as a reciter, or a recording you appear in. We confirm the deletion within seven days.

What we delete on request: access tokens tied to your account; your name from our posts' credits; recordings or clips you appear in, where you are the reciter. Posts already on the platforms are removed from the Bazm's own pages on request as well.

Questions about this process go to the same contacts.
